---
id: COM-002
title: No secret in code, commits, or logs
scope: company
mode: advisory
status: example
---

No password, API key, token, or private key is written into source code, a commit, a test fixture, or a log line. A secret is read at run time from the place the project keeps secrets.

## Why

A secret in a repo stays in its history and reaches every clone. A secret in a log reaches everyone who reads the log.

## How to check

The change adds no literal that looks like a credential, and no log line prints a token, a password, or a full request header.
