title: A change does what its story asks and no more
scope: company
mode: advisory
status: example
---

A change holds the work of one story. A refactor, a rename, or a cleanup that the story does not need goes into a change of its own.

## Why

A small change is reviewed well. A change that mixes a feature with unrelated edits hides the risky lines among the harmless ones.

## How to check

Every changed file can be explained by the story. An edit that cannot is moved out, or the developer says why it must stay.
