title: A new dependency is a team decision
scope: company
mode: advisory
status: example
---

A new third-party library or SDK is added only together with a decision record that names what it is for, what else was considered, and who keeps it up to date.

## Why

Every dependency is code the team did not write and still has to trust, update, and ship.

## How to check

A change to the dependency list comes with a decision record in the team repo, or with a link to one that is already accepted.
